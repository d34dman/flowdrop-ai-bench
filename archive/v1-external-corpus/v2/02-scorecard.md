# Scorecard

Every table below is generated by `python3 docs/drupalcon/data/tables_v2.py` from
[`../data/runs_v2.csv`](../data/runs_v2.csv). Regenerate rather than edit.

**How to read the matrix.** One letter per draw. A cell with several letters was run
several times. `·` means the cell was never run. The four models are columns; B7 ran on
the two Sonnets only.

**What to take from it.**

- **Seven silent failures, all in variants that let the model decide.** B4 has three, B2
  one (a note the model appended, with marks in it), B5 one, B8 one. B3's one is a Haiku
  truncation on the large page. B6, B7 and B9 have none.
- **Opus 5 is the only model with no silent and no loud failure**: 18 of 21 correct, two
  degraded, one format failure (B2 small, like every model). It is also the most expensive
  column.
- **B3 is correct 15 times out of 16**, on every model and page except Haiku on the large
  page. B9, the critic loop, is correct 11 of 15 with three loud failures on the first
  sweep (a FlowDrop bug, fixed the same day) and none silent.
- **The B7 and B8 medium rows are `degraded` for one reason**: the title heading
  `# Drupal versus WordPress` is left readable while the body is redacted, on Sonnet 4.6,
  Sonnet 5 and Opus 5 alike. B9 Sonnet 4.6 has the same leak; its critic accepted the draft
  unchanged. The other B9 models redact the title.
- **The small page is a false-positive control and it caught one**: B4 Sonnet 4.6 wrote
  six competitor names into a page that had none.
- **The large page grades fidelity and cost.** Its only competitor-ish name is neutral.
  Every `S` in that column is truncation or an appended note, not a redaction miss.
