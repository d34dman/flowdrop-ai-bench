# Comparison of Content Management Systems

Content Management Systems (CMS) are essential tools for building and maintaining websites. While there are many options available, Drupal is often praised for its power and flexibility. However, depending on your needs, other platforms might be more suitable.

## Drupal

Drupal is a free and open-source CMS written in PHP. It is designed for users who need a highly scalable and customizable system. Drupal is often used for large-scale corporate websites and government portals because of its robust security and permission systems.
