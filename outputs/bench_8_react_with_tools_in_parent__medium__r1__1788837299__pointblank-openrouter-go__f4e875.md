## Drupal: The Enterprise Powerhouse

Drupal is known for its robustness and flexibility. It is an open-source CMS designed for developers and enterprises that need to manage complex data structures and high volumes of traffic.

Key advantages of Drupal include:
* **Granular Access Control:** Detailed permissions for different user roles.
* **Multilingual Support:** Built-in capabilities to handle content in multiple languages.
* **Strong Community:** A vast ecosystem of modules that extend functionality.
